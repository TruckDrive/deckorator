**Decorate your Anki decks.** Deckorator gives the deck list, overview and study screen a clean, modern look, and it stays quiet while you study.

![Deck list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TruckDrive/deckorator/main/screenshots/home-dark.png)

### Features

- **8 presets** or your own accent color, or take it from your **system accent color** or **desktop wallpaper**
- **Backgrounds:** spotlight, dot grid, line grid, grain, waves, your own image, or your **desktop wallpaper** (re-synced on every start; live and video wallpapers become a still image)
- **Calm study mode:** while reviewing, the background stays quiet so the card comes first
- **Readable review counters** and tinted answer buttons
- **"Studied today" highlighted** on the home screen
- **Review Heatmap support:** fully restyled, colors only, or left untouched
- **Performance mode:** blur is switched off automatically on weak laptops
- **Settings window with a live preview of your own decks and cards**

### How to use

**Tools → Deckorator – Theme settings…** or the **Config** button in the add-on manager.

### Good to know

- Only changes how Anki looks. Your cards and note types are never modified.
- Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
- Found a problem? Open **Settings → Advanced → Copy diagnostic report** and post it on [GitHub](https://github.com/TruckDrive/deckorator/issues). That helps me fix it quickly.
