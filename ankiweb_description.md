**Decorate your Anki decks.** Deckorator gives the deck list, overview and study screen a clean, modern look, and it stays quiet while you study.

![Deck list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TruckDrive/deckorator/main/screenshots/home-lofi.png)

### Features

- **46 theme packs**, including the Omarchy color themes: a complete look in one click, illustrated or with photo wallpapers
- **Illustrated wallpapers** in your accent color, plus a **banner in the top bar**
- **My designs**: save your look, or turn any wallpaper into a complete theme (accent and tones adapt), share as a .deckorator file
- **Add-on center**: your add-ons as cards with a switch, settings and page link
- **Plays well with other add-ons**: their content below the deck list gets a matching card; other theme add-ons are detected
- **Online gallery**: browse wallpapers from Omarchy and ML4W on GitHub and turn them into designs
- **Card designs**: six styles for any note type (clean, index card, minimal, focus, study notes, like my theme), previewed on your own cards and fully reversible
- **Every area adjustable**: bold, subtle or Anki's original, per window
- **8 color presets** or your own accent color, or take it from your **system accent color** or **desktop wallpaper**
- **Backgrounds:** spotlight, dot grid, line grid, grain, waves, your own image, or your **desktop wallpaper** (re-synced on every start; live and video wallpapers become a still image)
- **Calm study mode:** while reviewing, the background stays quiet so the card comes first
- **Readable review counters** and tinted answer buttons
- **"Studied today" highlighted** on the home screen
- **Review Heatmap support:** fully restyled, colors only, or left untouched
- **Performance mode:** blur is switched off automatically on weak laptops
- **Card browser, editor, statistics, deck options, preferences and add-ons window** in the same style
- **Settings window with a live preview of your own decks and cards**

### How to use

**Tools → Deckorator – Theme settings…** or the **Config** button in the add-on manager.

### Good to know

- Only changes how Anki looks and never touches your cards. Card designs add a marked, removable CSS block to the note types you choose.
- Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
- Found a problem? Open **Settings → Advanced → Copy diagnostic report** and post it on [GitHub](https://github.com/TruckDrive/deckorator/issues). That helps me fix it quickly.
